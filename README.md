**AI-Powered Sri Lankan Tea Auction Platform**

A digital tea auction platform designed to modernize Sri Lankan tea auction operations using AI and data analytics.

**Technologies:**

`Next.js` `FastAPI` `MSSQL` `Python` `AI` `WebSockets` `JWT`

**Key Features:**

* 🤖 AI-powered tea blend creation
* 📊 Market and export analytics
* 💬 Natural-language database queries
* 🔨 Digital tea auctions and live bidding
* 📈 Auction and market dashboards
* 🔐 Secure authentication and role-based access
* 💳 Payment and transaction management
* 📦 Order tracking
* 💬 Buyer-seller communication
